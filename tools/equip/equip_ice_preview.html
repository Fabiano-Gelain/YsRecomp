Reflection on the ice

Preview to approve before it goes into the game. On the ice of Darm Tower the game itself draws the reflection along with Adol: seven rows under his feet, taken from Adol himself upside down, each color swapped for a lighter one. Here the same rule applies to the new pieces. Pick the pieces: the four Adols walk as in the game. Beside them, the game's original ice frames, for comparison.
