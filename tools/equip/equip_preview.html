Equipment on Adol

Preview to approve before it goes into the game. The colors, the swords and the shields (each with its own shape and size) are new drawings, made by hand in the style of small Adol from the equipment screen icons (the game's icon appears next to each choice, when the game showed it in a recorded session); hair, face and steps are the game's own. Pick the pieces: the four Adols walk as in the game (8 steps per second), with the shield held still on the arm. Beside them, the game's original Adol, for comparison.
